1.1 Bismillah

Beginning with the name of Allah is the habit of the believer in every good deed.

প্রতিটি ভালো কাজ আল্লাহর নামে শুরু করা মুমিনের অভ্যাস।

بِسْمِ
In the name নামে
ٱللَّهِ
of Allah আল্লাহর
ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ
the Most Gracious পরম করুণাময়
ٱلرَّحِيمِ
the Most Merciful অসীম দয়ালু

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

পরম করুণাময় অসীম দয়ালু আল্লাহর নামে (শুরু করছি)।

Quran 1:1

1.3 Morning Dua

A short dua said in the morning, acknowledging that everything is by the will of Allah.

ٱللَّهُمَّ
O Allah
بِكَ
by You
أَصْبَحْنَا
we have reached the morning
وَبِكَ
and by You
أَمْسَيْنَا
we reach the evening

O Allah, by You we have reached the morning, and by You we reach the evening.

وَبِكَ
and by You
نَحْيَا
we live
وَبِكَ
and by You
نَمُوتُ
we die
وَإِلَيْكَ
and to You
ٱلنُّشُورُ
is the resurrection

By You we live and by You we die, and to You is the resurrection.

Sunan at-Tirmidhi 3391
