## Bismillah

> Beginning with the name of Allah is the habit of the believer in every good deed.
> bn: প্রতিটি ভালো কাজ আল্লাহর নামে শুরু করা মুমিনের অভ্যাস।

| بِسْمِ | ٱللَّهِ | ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ | ٱلرَّحِيمِ |
|---|---|---|---|
| In the name | of Allah | the Most Gracious | the Most Merciful |
| নামে | আল্লাহর | পরম করুণাময় | অসীম দয়ালু |

en: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.
bn: পরম করুণাময় অসীম দয়ালু আল্লাহর নামে (শুরু করছি)।

ref: Quran 1:1

## Ayatul Kursi (excerpt)

> The opening lines of Ayatul Kursi, shown here as a layout sample.

| ٱللَّهُ | لَآ | إِلَـٰهَ | إِلَّا | هُوَ | ٱلْحَىُّ | ٱلْقَيُّومُ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Allah | there is no | God | but | He | the Ever Living | the One Who Sustains |

en: Allah! There is none worthy of worship but He, the Ever Living, the One Who Sustains and Protects all that exists.

| لَا | تَأْخُذُهُۥ | سِنَةٌۭ | وَلَا | نَوْمٌۭ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not | overtakes Him | slumber | and not | sleep |

en: Neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him.

ref: Al-Baqarah 2:255

## Morning Dua

> A short dua said in the morning, acknowledging that everything is by the will of Allah.

| ٱللَّهُمَّ | بِكَ | أَصْبَحْنَا | وَبِكَ | أَمْسَيْنَا |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O Allah | by You | we have reached the morning | and by You | we reach the evening |

en: O Allah, by You we have reached the morning, and by You we reach the evening.

| وَبِكَ | نَحْيَا | وَبِكَ | نَمُوتُ | وَإِلَيْكَ | ٱلنُّشُورُ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| and by You | we live | and by You | we die | and to You | is the resurrection |

en: By You we live and by You we die, and to You is the resurrection.

ref: Sunan at-Tirmidhi 3391
